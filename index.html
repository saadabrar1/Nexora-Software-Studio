    Ask us anything.

    Can’t find your answer? Message us on Upwork and we’ll reply within one business day.

    How much does a project cost?

    Every project gets a fixed quote after a one-week blueprint, so you know the price before we build. Book a call and you’ll have a plan and a number within 48 hours.

    Who owns the code?

    You do. We work in your repository from day one and hand over everything, including docs and deployment, at launch.

    Which time zones do you cover?

    We’re in Pakistan (GMT+5). Our working day overlaps with UK and EU hours, and with US mornings.

    How will I know the project is on track?

    You get a demo video every week showing what was built, plus access to the repo and the task board at any time.

    Can you work with our existing team?

    Yes. With the dedicated team model we join your Slack, Jira and GitHub and work like part of your company.

    What happens after launch?

    We stay on for fixes, new features and scaling, either monthly or whenever you need us.
